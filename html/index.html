Since 1998

W E L C O M E

We opened with six tables and one grill. Most of that is
still true: the room is bigger, but everything still comes off the same fire, and the menu is still written the morning it is served.

Produce comes from growers we have used for years. What they have decided is ready is what you will find on the menu that week.
